Tonight, Stated

The old screen made you author a schedule every time you opened it: seven day chips, a time, a Set button. This one states what will happen tomorrow morning and lets you bend it. Tap the big time. Try the scenarios. Everything on the phone is live.

Scenarios

What changed, and why

The day Every screen names the date. A weekday alarm tested at 01:00 on a Sunday did not ring, was correct, and read as a broken app, because the screen said 08:00 and nothing else.
Three verbs Change the routine, bend one date, skip one date. Tapping the time asks which. An override is keyed to a date, so moving Saturday leaves Sunday and next Saturday alone.
Skip is one tap Because the alternative is switching a routine off and forgetting, which is the oversleep every other rule here exists to prevent. It expires by itself.
Sentences Not green ticks. A tick meant "the server returned 200", and it took most of a night to learn how little that is worth. Only the phone may say will ring. Only an absolute instant from a server may say confirmed. Whoop says saved, permanently.
Ranges Eight Sleep's smart alarm fires in the 30 minutes before; Whoop's in the 60 minutes before. Drawing either as a single time is a lie about the one thing this app exists to tell you.
Refusals A leg that cannot be written says what it still holds, so the failure is a known time rather than an unknown one.
