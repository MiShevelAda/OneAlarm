Before you start

You need one alarm in the Eight Sleep app

Why it works this way

OneAlarm changes the alarm you already have rather than creating a new one. That keeps your vibration, thermal and level settings exactly as you set them, because it never has to guess what they should be.

Open the Eight Sleep app, make sure one alarm exists, then come back.

Failure states

Each one says what happened, whose fault it is, and what to do. None of them says "something went wrong".

Subscription inactive

Your password worked. Eight Sleep's alarms need an active subscription.
Nothing in OneAlarm can change this. Your other devices will still be set.

No alarm to move

No alarm found in your Eight Sleep account.
Open the Eight Sleep app, create one alarm at any time, then connect again.

Wrong password

Eight Sleep did not accept that email and password.
Your previously saved password was not overwritten, so a typo here cannot lock you out.

Rate limited

Too many attempts. Wait about a minute.
OneAlarm will not retry on its own, because repeated failed sign ins are how accounts get locked.

Whoop

Read this before linking Whoop

This one is different from the other two, and you should decide with the facts.

Whoop offers no official way in
OneAlarm uses the same private service their own app uses. Their terms let them act against an account doing this. Realistically that means your membership.
You will sign in again about monthly
Their login expires roughly every thirty days and there is no way to warn you before it does.
Keep the Whoop app installed
OneAlarm changes the alarm on Whoop's servers. Their app is what carries it to the band on your wrist.
Your bed and your phone do not depend on this. Skipping Whoop costs you one nudge, nothing else.

What amber means

Whoop took the change but did not report the time back.
The alarm is very likely set. OneAlarm will not colour it green because it could not read the answer, and claiming a confirmation it does not have is how an app loses your trust at 06:00.

The other amber case

Accepted, but it reads back as 08:50 rather than 07:50.
Almost always a timezone stored on their side that does not match yours. Worth opening their app.

What can never be sent

Each connection is limited to a short list of allowed addresses. Changing the bed temperature, running the pump, moving the frame and deleting anything are all refused before they leave the phone, including by mistake.

Edge cases

Two cases that are wrong by hours rather than obviously broken, so the design has to make them visible.

An offset crossing midnight

23:55
Eight Sleep
Night before
Waking at 00:05 means the bed starts at 23:55 the previous evening. Its days shift back by one so the bed warms on the right night.

A clock change between devices

These alarms straddle a daylight saving change and carry different UTC offsets. The real gap between first and last is 130 minutes, not the 70 you set.

Your iPhone alarm is off. It is the only one that rings with no account, no network and no subscription. Everything else is a nudge.
